EXPLORATORY OFFLINE PUBLIC DATA NOT OUR MOTOR

Fault detection was tested offline on the HUST bearing dataset (real 750 W induction-motor bench, artificial 0.2 mm bearing cracks, 51.2 kHz vibration). Every bearing type was held out of training in turn. Bearing type is used as the specimen identity; the dataset does not publish per-bearing serial numbers.
