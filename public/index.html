Dynamic Mathematical Formulations & Physical Rationales

In-Place Evaluation
1. Dynamic RMS Vibration Energy ISO 20816
RMS = √[ Σ (a_dyn[i])² / N ]
RMS = √(0.000088 / 256) = 0.0094 g
Directly quantifies continuous mechanical kinetic energy and destructive stress. Elevates during severe unbalance, misalignment, and structural looseness.
2. Vibration Kurtosis (4th Standardized Moment) Bearing Shock
κ = μ&sub4; / σ&sup4; = [ Σ (a_dyn - μ)&sup4; / N ] / [ Σ (a_dyn - μ)² / N ]²
κ = 0.00000002 / (0.000088)² = 4.53 (Nominal ∼ 3.0)
Measures tailedness of acceleration distribution. Early microscopic raceway spalls produce impulsive shocks that spike kurtosis > 5 long before RMS rises.
3. Crest Factor (Shock-to-Energy Ratio) Spall Impact
CF = Peak_dyn / RMS_dyn
CF = 0.0280 g / 0.0094 g = 2.98
Ratio of transient shock severity to continuous energy. Detects localized impact faults before broad-spectrum vibration rises.
4. Shewhart SPC Statistical Z-Score Low-Power Gate
Z = (RMS_dyn - μ&sub0;) / σ&sub0;
Z = (0.0094 - 0.080) / 0.025 = -2.82 σ (Threshold: > 2.0 σ)
Statistical process control gatekeeper. Instead of wasting CPU continuously running ML models, Stage 1 gates Stage 2: complex inference triggers only on anomaly breach.
5. Thermal Derivative (Rate of Temperature Change) Runaway Alert
ΔT/Δt = (T_now - T_past) / (Δt / 60) [°C/min]
ΔT = -0.10 °C over 15.0 s → -0.40 °C/min
Detects bearing lubrication starvation and winding insulation degradation 15-30 minutes in advance, without waiting for motor to overheat.
6. Acoustic Sound Pressure & Zero Crossing Rate Friction / Squeal
SPL = 120 + 20·log&sub1;&sub0;(RMS_norm) | ZCR = Σ |sgn(s[k]) - sgn(s[k-1])| / 2M
SPL = 120 + 20·log10(0.0022) = 66.8 dB | ZCR = 0.000
Calibrated airborne acoustic power. Metal-on-metal rubbing and dry bearing squeals generate high-frequency acoustics, spiking ZCR > 0.25.
